## Assembly notes

The two fabs are served differently:

* **JLCPCB** — only the 16 SMD positions, solder THT yourself
  (`fan-controller-bom.csv`, `fan-controller-cpl.csv`)
* **PCBWay** — all 23 positions including the through-hole parts
  (`pcbway/`), since PCBWay offers through-hole assembly

**JLCPCB only assembles the 16 SMD positions.** The 7 through-hole designators (C1, J1, J2–J5, U1) are
removed from BOM and CPL — their catalogue does not carry them. They are sourced separately and soldered by
hand: specifications and quantities in **THT-ORDER-LIST.md**.

**U1 is the socket position, not the module.** Two **1×7 female headers** belong there; the XIAO module
itself is plugged in later and is not part of the assembly. The PCBWay BOM therefore says **Qty 2** — the
column means pieces per board, not number of designators. A hint in the Value field is not enough; the
calculation follows the column.

The reason for this twist: the sockets have no schematic symbol of their own — their holes are the 14
through-hole pads of the module footprint. As a BOM line of their own with invented designators (U1-SKT1/2)
they would have no CPL entry, and JLCPCB rejects BOM lines without a matching CPL entry. That is why the
position runs under U1, with the socket as the comment.

All parts are on the top side. J1 (barrel jack) overhangs the right board edge by design — its flange seats
on the edge so the socket is reachable from outside an enclosure.

The **LCSC Part #** column in the BOM is empty for some lines. Either fill it in yourself or let the
assembler substitute from stock — apart from the barrel jack and the AMS1117, everything is standard stock.

BOM and CPL are in **JLCPCB's column format**. kicad-cli exports its own headers
(`Ref,Val,Package,PosX,PosY,Rot,Side`, or `Value` instead of `Comment`), which their import rejects — which is
why `make_bom.py` and `make_cpl.py` convert the files. For PCBWay or Aisler both formats are generally fine.

JLCPCB matches **BOM and CPL designator by designator**. The BOM therefore contains complete comma lists
(`R1,R2,R3,R4`) instead of ranges (`R1-R4`), and every designator exists in both files. Currently 23 on both
sides, congruent.

## Reproducing

```sh
fan-controller/scripts/make_fab.sh
```

Gerbers must be exported with an explicit `--layers` list. A bare `kicad-cli pcb export gerbers` writes
*every* layer, including the `User.Eco1` scratch layer used during layout for review markers.

Checked when this repository was assembled: regenerating from the current board and schematic reproduces the
tracked BOM and CPL files byte for byte; ERC and DRC report 0 violations and 0 unconnected pads.

## LCSC part numbers

JLCPCB's matching guesses without a part number and usually does not find THT parts at all. The numbers are
therefore kept in `scripts/make_bom.py` (dict `LCSC`).

Confirmed — supplied by JLCPCB's own BOM matching, all Basic Parts:

| Pos | Part | LCSC |
|---|---|---|
| C2 | 10uF 0805 | C440198 |
| D1 | SS34 SMA | C8678 |
| U2 | AMS1117-5.0 SOT-223 | C6187 |
| R1-R4 | 10k 0603 | C25804 |
| R5-R8 | 1k 0603 | C21190 |
| R9-R12 | 330 0603 | C23138 |

Researched, **not** confirmed by JLCPCB — check against the catalogue before ordering:

| Pos | Part | LCSC | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | SMD1812P150TF/24 | C209713 | 1.5A **24V**. The /8 variant (C209721) is only 8V and not enough for 12V. |
| U1 | female header 2.54mm 1x40 | C5303 | to be cut to 2x 1x7 |

Open — to be selected in the web interface:

* **J1** barrel jack 5.5x2.1mm horizontal
* **J2-J5** pin header 1x4 2.54mm
* **C1** 220uF electrolytic cap, **leaded**, pitch 3.5mm

On C1: JLCPCB suggested C2941234, an **SMD** electrolytic cap 6.3x5.8mm. It does not fit — our footprint has
leaded pads with 3.5mm pitch. Do not accept the suggestion.

JLCPCB reports the three resistor lines as "Unconfirmed" because the comment ("10k") is not the manufacturer
part number. The parts are right in substance; confirm in the web interface, otherwise the quantity stays at 0.
